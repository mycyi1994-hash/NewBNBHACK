| D-23 | **Agent proposal (9/27 full audit)**: rules for recording and settling money. (1) The effects of deposits, redeems and swaps are applied as soon as they confirm, in one transaction that locks the plan row (`FOR UPDATE`), inserting the receipt first (`packages/db/src/record.ts`) — receipt hashes are unique in a single lowercase form, and cycle snapshots do not overwrite the money columns. (2) Outbox settlement (reconcile with the chain → finish the cycle that was waiting → apply out-of-cycle txs, `settleOutbox`) runs **regardless of mode** on every tick and right before every signature. (3) A dead cycle is cleaned up by the plan's next lock holder: if something was signed, it is finished from the chain; if not, FAILED `INTERRUPTED` + reservation released + next run time. If the nonce was used but we have no receipt, or a swap older than 10 minutes was lost by the node, it is not guessed to be FAILED: it stays PENDING + a human alert after 30 minutes (RUNBOOK §3.4). (5) A full position redeem (stop, guardian, expiry, operator) is done only while holding the plan lock and only when the plan has no unsettled tx.
